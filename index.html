01 — The Idea

What is ALIGN?

More Than Performance

Student athletes are often defined by times, scores, grades, and accomplishments. ALIGN looks beyond those measurements to explore the person behind them.

Finding Balance

Athletics can shape confidence, identity, friendships, goals, and everyday life. Finding balance means creating space for every part of who you are.

03

Friendships, interests, personality, values, and discovering who you are outside of sport.

03 — Beyond

Who Are You Beyond the Finish Line?

Being a student athlete is only one part of a much bigger story. ALIGN encourages athletes to recognize their identity, protect their well-being, pursue their goals, and build a future that extends beyond sport.
